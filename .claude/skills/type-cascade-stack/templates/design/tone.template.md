## Five design principles (non-negotiable)

1. **Clarity over cleverness** — every screen scannable in 2 seconds.
2. **Calm confidence** — interface lowers anxiety, never raises it.
3. **Precision without coldness** — clinical accuracy + warm spacing.
4. **Earn every pixel** — no decoration without function.
5. **Accessible by default** — WCAG AA is the floor.

## What this tone forbids

- Decorative gradients
- Cartoon illustration
- Gamification badges
- Aggressive "marketing" copy ("Don't miss out!")
- Ambiguous icons without labels
- Animations that don't serve orientation
